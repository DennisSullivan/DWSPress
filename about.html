About DWS Press

DWS Press was established to preserve and publish historical memoirs, family histories, photographs, maps, letters, journals, and other archival materials collected over multiple generations of the Sullivan family.

The press was created to ensure that original voices and firsthand experiences remain available to future readers, researchers, historians, and descendants.

Mission

Our mission is to preserve authentic historical accounts and present them in forms that are accessible to modern readers.

Wherever possible, published works are based on original source documents, memoirs, photographs, letters, maps, and other contemporary records.

The Sullivan Family Archive

The Sullivan Family Archive contains military memoirs, travel narratives, correspondence, maps, photographs, family histories, and other historical records.

Many of these materials were created or preserved by William John Sullivan and Lois Welling Sullivan and have been maintained within the family for decades.

Current Publications

  • Navigator — World War II memoir of William John Sullivan
  • Chief — autobiographical writings and recollections
  • Ocho — the story of a six-month family journey through Mexico in 1958 (in development)
