About DWS Press

DWS Press was established to preserve and publish the writings, artwork, photographs, maps, letters, memoirs, and historical records of the Sullivan Family Archive.

At the heart of the archive are the lives and creative works of William John Sullivan and Lois Welling Sullivan, whose writings, correspondence, photographs, artwork, and personal histories span much of the twentieth century.

What began as a family preservation effort has gradually evolved into a publishing program dedicated to making these materials available to a wider audience.

The Sullivan Family Archive

The archive includes military memoirs, travel narratives, family histories, photographs, maps, journals, letters, artwork, and other historical materials preserved over multiple generations.

These collections document military service, family life, travel, artistic work, and everyday experiences that might otherwise have been lost with time.

Authors and Creators

William John Sullivan authored works including Navigator and Chief.

Lois Welling Sullivan's published works include Arizona Rose and Conjugating Love, drawing upon her autobiographical writings, letters, artwork, and watercolors.

Additional publications drawn from the archive are planned, including Ocho and future family works.

Current and Future Publications

  • Navigator — A World War II memoir by William John Sullivan
  • Chief — Autobiographical writings by William John Sullivan
  • Arizona Rose — Autobiography of Lois Welling Sullivan
  • Conjugating Love — Writings and watercolors by Lois Welling Sullivan
  • Ocho — A six-month family journey through Mexico in 1958
  • Children's Book — Forthcoming work by Sheila Polk

Our Purpose

DWS Press exists to preserve authentic voices and firsthand experiences for future generations. By transforming family archives into published books, we seek to ensure that personal histories remain part of the historical record.
