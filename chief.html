Chief

The Autobiography of William John Sullivan

By William John Sullivan

Chief is the autobiography of William John Sullivan, tracing his journey from childhood in Duluth through World War II service, marriage to Lois Welling Sullivan, the raising of a large family, years in the construction industry, extensive travel, and a lifetime of adventures.

Chief

"We had fun."

Late in life, Bill Sullivan often summarized his life with these three simple words. They capture the spirit of this memoir: family, adventure, friendship, travel, and a lifetime of stories.

About the Book

Told with humor, honesty, and a remarkable eye for detail, Chief follows Bill Sullivan through nearly every stage of life, from childhood memories and wartime service to marriage, parenthood, travel, retirement, and family traditions.

The memoir records decades of camping trips, road trips, adventures throughout the American Southwest and Mexico, friendships, family stories, and the experiences that shaped the Sullivan family.

A Life Remembered

Chief presents far more than dates and events. It records the people, places, decisions, challenges, and opportunities that influenced Bill throughout his life.

Readers encounter both ordinary and extraordinary experiences through the eyes of someone who lived through much of the twentieth century.

Relationship to Navigator

While Navigator focuses primarily on military service during World War II, Chief provides a broader view of Bill's entire life.

Together, the two books create a rich portrait of the man, his family, and the events that shaped his generation.

The Sullivan Family Archive

Chief is one of the foundational works preserved within the Sullivan Family Archive.

The memoir also serves as an important source document for later projects, providing family history, context, and personal recollections not found elsewhere.
