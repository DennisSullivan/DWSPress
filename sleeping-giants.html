Sleeping Giants

A Children's Story

By Sheila Sullivan Polk

About the Book

Sleeping Giants is a forthcoming children's book by Sheila Sullivan Polk that combines imagination, storytelling, and adventure.

Through the eyes of young characters, readers are invited into a world where ordinary landscapes can hold extraordinary secrets and where curiosity leads to discovery.

The story encourages children to look beyond first appearances and to find wonder in the world around them.

For Young Readers

Sleeping Giants is written for children and families who enjoy stories filled with mystery, exploration, and imagination.

Themes of curiosity, courage, friendship, and discovery help create a reading experience that is both entertaining and meaningful.

Words and Art

As with Sheila Sullivan Polk's other work, storytelling and illustration work together to bring the narrative to life.

The visual elements are designed to encourage young readers to engage with the story and imagine themselves as part of the adventure.

Publication Status

Sleeping Giants is currently in development by DWS Press.

Additional details, artwork, and publication information will be announced as the project progresses.
