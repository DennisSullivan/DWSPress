William John Sullivan

William John Sullivan served as a navigator in the United States Army Air Forces during World War II. His wartime experiences in training, combat, and long-range operations in the South Pacific became the foundation for his memoir Navigator.

Throughout his life he preserved photographs, documents, maps, letters, and personal writings that now form part of the Sullivan Family Archive.

His published works include Navigator and Chief.

The Archive

The Sullivan Family Archive preserves memoirs, letters, photographs, maps, travel narratives, and family history materials collected and maintained over multiple generations.

DWS Press was established to preserve and publish these materials for future readers, researchers, historians, and family members.
