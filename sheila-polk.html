Sheila Sullivan Polk

Sheila Sullivan Polk is a writer, storyteller, illustrator, and contributor to the continuing publishing program of DWS Press.

Her work reflects the next generation of the Sullivan family’s creative tradition and focuses on stories written for children, families, and future generations.

Storyteller

Sheila's writing combines imagination, family values, humor, and adventure. Her stories are rooted in the belief that reading should entertain while encouraging curiosity, creativity, and kindness.

Her books were originally created for children and grandchildren, preserving family stories and experiences in forms that younger readers can enjoy.

Illustrator

In addition to writing, Sheila creates artwork and illustrations that bring her stories to life.

Her combination of words and pictures reflects the close connection between storytelling and visual art that runs throughout the Sullivan family archive.

Publications

  • The Adventures of Three Brothers — children's adventure story
  • Sleeping Giants — forthcoming children's book

Continuing the Tradition

While DWS Press preserves the writings, artwork, photographs, and historical records of earlier generations, it also supports the creation of new works by contemporary members of the Sullivan family.

Sheila's books demonstrate how family stories, creativity, and artistic expression continue to evolve while remaining connected to the broader mission of preserving and sharing the Sullivan family legacy.
