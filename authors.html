Authors

DWS Press publishes and preserves works by members of the Sullivan family, including memoirs, autobiographies, historical works, travel narratives, poetry, artwork, and children's literature.

William John Sullivan

William John Sullivan served as a navigator in the United States Army Air Forces during World War II. His writings include memoirs, autobiographical works, travel narratives, family history, and artwork created over a lifetime of military service, family life, travel, and personal reflection.

Major works include Navigator, Chief, Ocho, and Glimpses of the Good and the Beautiful.

Lois Welling Sullivan

Lois Welling Sullivan was a writer, artist, poet, and family historian whose work preserved memories of Arizona, family life, travel, faith, and personal growth. Her publications frequently combine writing, photographs, and original artwork.

Major works include Arizona Rose, Conjugating Love, and portions of Ocho.

Sheila Sullivan Polk

Sheila Sullivan Polk is the author of children's stories and creative family publications that encourage imagination, exploration, and a love of reading. Her work often combines storytelling, family collaboration, and original illustration.

Major works include The Adventures of Three Brothers and Sleeping Giants.
