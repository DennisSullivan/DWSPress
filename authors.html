Authors

DWS Press publishes works drawn from the Sullivan Family Archive and related family collections. The archive contains memoirs, travel narratives, autobiographical writings, photographs, maps, letters, artwork, and family history materials created over multiple generations.

Our authors represent different voices, experiences, and perspectives while sharing a commitment to preserving authentic personal history.

William John Sullivan

World War II Army Air Forces navigator, memoirist, traveler, and family historian.

His works include Navigator, a memoir of wartime service in the South Pacific, and Chief, an autobiographical account of his life and experiences.

He is also one of the principal authors behind the developing publication project Ocho.

Lois Welling Sullivan

Writer, artist, correspondent, autobiographer, and keeper of family history.

Her published works include Arizona Rose, her autobiography, and Conjugating Love, a collection of writings and artwork drawn from her journals, reflections, and watercolors.

Her letters and travel writings are also central source material for the continuing development of Ocho.
