Arizona Rose

The Childhood of Lois Elaine Welling

By Lois Welling Sullivan

Arizona Rose is the autobiography of Lois Elaine Welling Sullivan. Drawing on journals, memories, family photographs, and archival materials, the book recreates her childhood in Phoenix, Arizona, during the 1920s and 1930s.

About the Book

Through Lois's own words, readers encounter a rapidly growing desert city surrounded by citrus groves, irrigation canals, ranches, neighborhood communities, and family businesses.

The story preserves everyday experiences, family traditions, friendships, education, recreation, and the rich social history of an earlier Arizona.

Historical Value

Beyond autobiography, Arizona Rose is a record of life in early twentieth-century Arizona.

The book includes family history, photographs, local stories, descriptions of businesses, schools, churches, neighborhoods, and the people who helped shape the Phoenix community.

It offers readers a firsthand account of a way of life that has largely disappeared from the modern Southwest.

Publication History

Arizona Rose was developed from the writings, journals, photographs, and personal papers of Lois Welling Sullivan preserved within the Sullivan Family Archive.

The first edition was produced as a family heirloom edition through DWS Press.

Future editions may make this work available to a broader audience interested in Arizona history, family history, and memoir.
