Preserving and Publishing the Sullivan Family Archive

DWS Press publishes memoirs, history, travel narratives, poetry, and family stories drawn from a remarkable archive of letters, journals, photographs, artwork, maps, and personal writings spanning multiple generations.

Through carefully edited publications, historical materials are preserved and shared with readers, researchers, family historians, and future generations.

The Sullivan Family Archive

The archive preserves memoirs, journals, family correspondence, military records, photographs, paintings, maps, travel narratives, and historical documents collected and preserved across generations.

These materials form the foundation for the books, biographies, memoirs, and future projects published by DWS Press.
