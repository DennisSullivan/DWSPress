The Sullivan Family Archive

The Sullivan Family Archive is a growing collection of memoirs, letters, journals, photographs, artwork, maps, military records, travel narratives, and family history materials preserved by multiple generations of the Sullivan family.

DWS Press was established to preserve, organize, publish, and share selected portions of this archive through books and other historical projects.

William John Sullivan Collection

The William John Sullivan collection includes World War II records, memoirs, photographs, maps, correspondence, autobiographical writings, travel narratives, and family history materials.

These materials form the foundation for Navigator, Chief, and Ocho.

William John Sullivan

Lois Welling Sullivan Collection

The Lois Welling Sullivan collection includes autobiographical writings, journals, letters, photographs, artwork, watercolor paintings, family records, and travel materials.

These materials form the foundation for Arizona Rose, Conjugating Love, and portions of Ocho.

Lois Welling Sullivan

Sheila Sullivan Polk Collection

The Sheila Sullivan Polk collection includes children's stories, illustrations, family histories, and creative works written for younger readers.

These materials form the foundation for The Adventures of Three Brothers, Sleeping Giants, and future publications.

Preservation Efforts

Current preservation efforts include scanning, cataloging, transcribing, organizing, and digitally archiving historical materials.

Special attention is given to original source documents so that future generations may have access to authentic firsthand accounts and records.

Looking Ahead

The archive continues to grow as additional photographs, manuscripts, letters, and family records are located, digitized, and preserved.

Future DWS Press publications will continue to draw upon these collections, making important family stories and historical records available to a wider audience.
