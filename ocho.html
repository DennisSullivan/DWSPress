Ocho

A Family Journey Through Mexico

By William John Sullivan and Lois Welling Sullivan

About the Book

Ocho tells the story of an extraordinary six-month family journey through Mexico in 1958. Traveling with eight children, William and Lois Sullivan left Arizona to explore a country that was then largely unfamiliar to most American families.

The journey became an adventure filled with discovery, challenges, cultural encounters, unexpected events, and lasting memories.

Through letters, journals, photographs, and personal recollections, Ocho captures both the practical realities and the wonder of family travel during the mid-twentieth century.

A Family Adventure

Ocho is more than a travel narrative. It is a portrait of family life, resilience, curiosity, and the experience of seeing another culture through the eyes of both parents and children.

Along the way, the Sullivan family encountered people, places, traditions, and landscapes that left a lasting impression and shaped family memories for generations.

From the Archive

The book draws upon extensive original source material preserved within the Sullivan Family Archive.

Letters written home during the journey, journals, photographs, travel records, and later memoirs provide multiple perspectives on the same remarkable experience.

Together these sources create a richly detailed account of family travel in Mexico during a period of significant social and cultural change.

Publication Status

Ocho is currently in development by DWS Press.

The project combines the writings of William John Sullivan and Lois Welling Sullivan into a single narrative that preserves both voices while documenting one of the family's most memorable adventures.
