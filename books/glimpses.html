Glimpses of the Good and the Beautiful

Sculptures and Paintings

By William John Sullivan

Glimpses of the Good and the Beautiful is a retrospective collection of sculptures, paintings, drawings, and other artistic works created by William John Sullivan and compiled by Steven Sullivan.

About the Book

During his lifetime, William John Sullivan created numerous sculptures, paintings, drawings, and other works of art. Many of these works were given to family members and friends and eventually became scattered among private collections.

Recognizing the importance of preserving this artistic legacy, Steven Sullivan undertook the task of locating and photographing as many pieces as possible. The resulting collection brings together artwork from throughout Bill's life and provides a lasting record of his creativity, craftsmanship, and artistic vision.

Although not every work could be located, this volume preserves a significant portion of William John Sullivan's artistic output for future generations of the Sullivan family.
